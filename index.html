FAQ

Before you send footage

What software do you edit in?

Alight Motion and CapCut, both on mobile. It's a fully mobile workflow, start to final export.

How do I send you my footage?

A shared Google Drive or Dropbox folder works best for raw clips. For quick short-form jobs, WeTransfer is fine too.

How many revisions are included?

Two rounds of revisions come with every project. Anything beyond that is billed at a small hourly add-on.

What formats do you deliver in?

MP4 at whatever resolution and aspect ratio your platform needs — 9:16 for Shorts/Reels/TikTok, 16:9 for YouTube.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes — most of my client work comes through Upwork alongside local Ethiopian clients. Time zones aren't a problem.

How fast can you turn a video around?

Short-form edits typically land within 24–48 hours. Long-form videos depend on length and complexity — I'll always quote a timeline up front.
