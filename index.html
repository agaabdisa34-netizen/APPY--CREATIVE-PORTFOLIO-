FAQ

Property video, answered

What type of real estate videos do you create?

Property reels for single listings, walkthrough-style videos, developer and brand videos, and motion graphics overlays like maps and price or feature callouts.

Can you edit from raw property footage?

Yes — send over your raw clips (drone, walkthrough, phone footage) and I'll shape them into a finished video.

Can you create videos from property photos only?

Yes — a photo-only listing can still become a video, using motion, transitions and on-screen text to bring the space to life.

Can you add maps and location information?

Yes — location maps, neighborhood context, pricing and feature labels can all be added as animated graphics.

Can you create cinematic B-roll?

When real footage can't cover a shot, AI-assisted B-roll can fill the gap — used to support the story, never to misrepresent the property.

How do I send my footage?

A shared Google Drive or Dropbox folder works best for raw property footage.

How do revisions work?

You'll review a first cut and share feedback before the final version is delivered.

What platforms can you create videos for?

Vertical for Instagram Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts, and horizontal for YouTube, listing sites or a website.

Have a property worth showing?

Send over your footage and let's turn it into something people actually watch.
